## ⬇️ Download & install

1. Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download the **AsylumRP-Free** zip.
2. Right-click the zip → **Extract All…** and pick where to keep it.
3. Open the **AsylumRP-Free** folder. Inside you'll see:
   ```
   AsylumRP-Free/
   ├── AsylumRP-Free.exe            ← double-click this to start
   ├── (support files)              ← needed to run, don't move or delete them
   └── How to use AsylumRP Free.txt ← the full guide
   ```
4. Double-click **AsylumRP-Free.exe**.

> ⚠️ **Keep the whole AsylumRP-Free folder together.** If you want it on your desktop, make a shortcut instead of moving the exe out: right-click `AsylumRP-Free.exe` → **Send to → Desktop (create shortcut)**.

**You need:** Windows 10 or 11, the **Discord desktop app** (the browser version of Discord can't show custom statuses), and the **Asylum Resident** role in [Anime Asylum](https://discord.gg/anime-asylum). Every member gets that role.

### 🔑 Log in once
The first time you open AsylumRP Free, click **Log in with Discord** → **Authorize** in your browser. It checks that you're an Asylum Resident in Anime Asylum, then opens. Use the same Discord account as your Discord app.
AsylumRP Free only sees your username and your Anime Asylum roles: not your messages, servers or password. Your login stays on your PC.

### "Windows protected your PC"?
AsylumRP Free is new, so Windows may warn you the first time. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
This is a common false alarm for new programs that aren't signed with a paid certificate.

## 🚀 Setup (5 minutes)

**1. Make a Discord application**
- Go to the **[Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications)** and log in.
- Click **New Application**, give it a name and click **Create**.
- The name you choose is what shows after **"Playing"** on your profile (for example *Anime Asylum*).

**2. Copy the Application ID**
- On the **General Information** page, click **Copy** under **Application ID**.

**3. Set your status**
- Open AsylumRP Free and paste the ID into **Application ID**.
- Type your **Details** (line 1) and **State** (line 2).
- Check the **Live Preview** on the right.

**4. Connect**
- Make sure the Discord desktop app is open, then click **Connect**.
- The badge at the top says **● LIVE ON DISCORD** when it's working. 🎉

> 💡 **Not showing?** In Discord go to **User Settings → Activity Privacy** and turn on **Share your detected activities with others**.

For the full guide, open **How to use AsylumRP Free.txt** in your AsylumRP-Free folder (or **Help → Open User Guide** inside the app).

## 🖼️ Adding pictures

1. In the Developer Portal, open your application → **Rich Presence → Art Assets**.
2. Upload an image (at least 512×512) and give it a name, like `my_picture`.
3. Type that name into **Large image** or **Small image** in AsylumRP Free.

You can also paste any image link starting with `https://` instead. New uploads can take a few minutes to work.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **"You don't have the Asylum Resident role yet"** | Finish joining Anime Asylum (accept the rules / verify), wait a minute, then click **Check Again** |
| **"You've used your 5 hours for today"** | Your time comes back at midnight Pacific time. The app shows how long until then |
| **"{cpu} only works in the full AsylumRP"** | That variable is an Insane Elite feature. Take it out of your text to go live |
| **"Couldn't reach Discord"** | Open the Discord **desktop app** and try again |
| **Nothing shows on my profile** | Turn on **Activity Privacy → Share your detected activities**, and wait up to 15 seconds |
| **"Invalid Client ID"** | Re-copy the **Application ID** from the Developer Portal |
| **Picture doesn't show** | Wait a few minutes after uploading, and check the name matches exactly |
| **Program won't open** | Make sure `AsylumRP-Free.exe` is still inside the AsylumRP-Free folder with its support files |
| **Won't start with Windows** | Turn **Settings → Run When Windows Starts** off and back on, then open AsylumRP Free once |
| **Using Discord in a browser** | Custom statuses need the [Discord desktop app](https://discord.com/download). It can run in the background |

Still stuck? Ask in **[Anime Asylum](https://discord.gg/anime-asylum)**.

## 💾 Your settings

Everything you save is stored only on your computer:
```
C:\Users\<your name>\.asylumrp\profiles.json
```
Open it with **File → Open Settings Folder** inside AsylumRP Free.

**Upgrading to Insane Elite?** The full AsylumRP uses this same folder, so all your profiles are already there.

**Updating:** AsylumRP Free tells you when a new version is out. Click **Update Now** and it installs itself. Your profiles, settings and today's time are kept.

**To uninstall:** use **File → Uninstall AsylumRP Free…** inside the app. It offers to back up your setup first and asks whether to keep your profiles. Keep them if you're upgrading or also use the full AsylumRP.
