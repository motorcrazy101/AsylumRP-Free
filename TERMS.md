## 8. Privacy

AsylumRP Free is designed to keep your information on your computer:

- **No sign-up and no password.** AsylumRP Free never asks for your Discord password or token. You log in through Discord's own "Authorize" page, and it only receives your Discord user ID, username and Anime Asylum roles, to check your access.
- **Your login is saved only on your computer**, encrypted, in `C:\Users\<your name>\.asylumrp\access-free.dat`. Help → Log Out of AsylumRP Free deletes it.
- **Your daily time** is saved only on your computer, encrypted, in `.asylumrp\usage-free.dat` and in your Windows registry. To know when a new day starts, AsylumRP Free reads the date from Discord's servers. No information about you is sent for that.
- **Your settings are saved only on your computer**, in `C:\Users\<your name>\.asylumrp\profiles.json` (the full AsylumRP uses the same file, so your profiles carry over if you upgrade).
- **The Developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data.** AsylumRP Free has no analytics or tracking.
- AsylumRP Free talks to the **Discord app on your computer** to set your status. What you put in your status is then shown by Discord and handled under Discord's own privacy policy.
- To show the live preview, AsylumRP Free may load your Discord application's name and pictures from Discord's servers, and load any image links you enter, directly from those websites.
